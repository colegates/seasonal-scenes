# Changelog

## 1.0.2

- When the theme changes (autumn to scuba, say), leaves still in the air fade out instead of landing and lying under the bubbles for the rest of the slot. The same goes for an act from the old theme that is still finishing.
- Changing the preview theme in the card editor drops the old theme's act straight away.

## 1.0.1

- Install steps for the Home Assistant Marketplace (HACS built in from 2026.11).
- First release made after the HACS validation checks pass, as the HACS store requires.

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- 113 acts across 19 themes: four seasons, night time, eight holidays,
  birthdays and six hobbies (golf, rugby, French horn and bassoon, pottery,
  skiing, scuba diving).
- Card mode with a sky that follows the time of day, and overlay mode that
  plays along the bottom of the whole dashboard.
- Birthdays from the card config or a text helper, weather from any weather
  entity, bin nights, and boosts that bring a theme on while an entity is on.
- Southern hemisphere seasons, Home Assistant's time zone, a choice of
  holidays and hobbies, and a visual editor.
